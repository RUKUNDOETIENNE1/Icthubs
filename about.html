About ICTHubs

We build intelligence and execution systems that help people and organizations make better decisions and take effective action at scale.

Mission

To design and deploy intelligent technologies that turn fragmented data into meaningful insights, enable predictive and proactive decision-making, automate complex operational processes, improve efficiency and productivity, and empower organizations to act with confidence.

Vision

To become a global leader in intelligent systems that help humanity understand reality more clearly, anticipate challenges earlier, and coordinate effective action at scale.

What we do

ICTHubs develops AI-powered platforms, digital infrastructure, and intelligent execution systems that transform data into decisions and decisions into results. We create advanced software solutions powered by artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation, and intelligent workflows.

Founder story

ICTHubs was founded by Rukundo Etienne with a simple belief: technology should not merely digitize existing processes—it should amplify human potential.

From Kigali, Rwanda, Etienne envisioned a future where intelligent systems could help businesses, governments, and communities solve complex challenges more effectively. Frustrated by generic solutions that failed to address local realities, he began building platforms designed around context, adaptability, and long-term impact.

What started as an ambitious idea has grown into ICTHubs—a technology company dedicated to developing AI-powered systems, digital platforms, and operational infrastructure for the future. Guided by Etienne's vision and supported by a growing team of innovators, ICTHubs continues to build technologies that bridge today's challenges with tomorrow's possibilities.
