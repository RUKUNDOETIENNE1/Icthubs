Imboni Serve

A modern hospitality management platform designed for restaurants, hotels, cafés, and bars. It provides digital tools to run daily operations more efficiently, improve customer experience, and increase revenue. Features include POS systems, QR code menus and digital ordering, kitchen and order management, reservations and table management, staff and role management, inventory and stock tracking, sales analytics and reporting, Mobile Money/payment integrations, customer loyalty and feedback systems, multi-branch management, and real-time dashboards.

Tech used: React, Node.js, Supabase, Mobile Money APIs
Kitchen Display: Streamlined prep
Reservations: Manage tables & shifts
POS: Fast checkout & receipts
QR Ordering: Scan & pay
Impact: Faster service, higher table turnover, increased revenue, and better guest experience across venues.

AgriPal

Agricultural Intelligence for a Changing World

AgriPal combines AI, forecasting models, geospatial monitoring, and field intelligence to help agricultural organizations detect emerging risks, improve resilience, and make data-driven decisions at scale.

AI Intelligence Platform • Risk Forecasting • Satellite & Field Data • Decision Support Systems

Helping agriculture move from reaction to prediction.

Tech used: TensorFlow, FastAPI, Twilio, Supabase
Leaf Analysis: Disease/pest classification
Mobile Workflow: Capture → Detect → Advise
Impact: 800 cooperatives, 40% reduction in crop loss, 25% yield improvement across pilot regions.
